# 用 ChatGPT（GPT Image）生圖

每張圖**開一個新對話**，整段英文貼上。生好後下載 PNG，放到 `assets/raw/`，檔名照下面寫。
不滿意就在同一個對話說「再淡一點」「屋簷翹一點」之類的，讓它改。

---

## 1. `gate.png` — 拱門（最重要）

> Create an image. Size 1024x1536 (portrait). Transparent background PNG.
>
> A perfectly symmetrical, straight-on front orthographic view of an elegant Taiwanese Taoist daochang (道場) gate. It should feel serene, light and refined — not a heavy, dark temple and not an imperial palace.
> Double-eave roof with pale celadon jade-green glazed tiles, gently upturned eave tips, small refined ridge ornaments. Carved wooden brackets (dougong) under the eaves in light honey wood with pale jade and champagne-gold details.
> Ivory / warm off-white plastered walls and round pillars, thin champagne-gold trim.
> A light wooden horizontal plaque with a champagne-gold border centered above the doorway — completely blank, no characters.
> In the center, a tall round-topped arched doorway (semicircle top, straight vertical sides) with a slim gold arch frame. The doorway opening itself must be fully transparent (empty), so things behind it can show through.
> Three wide pale stone steps at the bottom.
> NO lanterns, no people, no text, no sky, no trees, no ground beyond the steps.
> Painterly cinematic realism, soft cool moonlight from the upper left, very fine detail.

## 2. `bg.png` — 瑤池背景

> Create an image. Size 1536x1024 (landscape).
>
> A mystical night landscape of the Jade Pool (瑤池) on the sacred Mount Kunlun from Chinese mythology. Layered misty mountains in refined Chinese ink-wash painting style, jade green and deep teal tones, luminous mist drifting between the peaks, a soft glowing moonlit haze in the center of the sky.
> In the lower foreground, a calm luminous jade-colored pool with gentle reflections.
> Blossoming peach trees with pale pink flowers framing the far left and far right edges.
> Keep the center of the image open (soft mist, distant mountains) because a building will be placed in the middle later.
> Cinematic, painterly, calm and sacred. No buildings, no people, no text.

## 3. `koi-1.png`、`koi-2.png`、`koi-3.png` — 鯉魚（一張一隻）

每次把 `[COLOR]` 換掉：

- koi-1：`red and white (kohaku)`
- koi-2：`shimmering gold`
- koi-3：`white with black and red patches (showa)`

> Create an image. Size 1536x1024 (landscape). Transparent background PNG.
>
> A single ornamental koi carp seen from directly above, as if looking straight down into water. The body is straight and horizontal, head pointing to the RIGHT, long flowing translucent butterfly fins and tail spread wide. Color: [COLOR].
> Traditional Chinese gongbi painting style, finely detailed scales, elegant.
> Only the fish — no water, no shadow, no ripples, no text.
